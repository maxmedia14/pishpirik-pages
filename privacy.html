PISHPIRIK Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

PISHPIRIK is a multiplayer card game.

Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google or Facebook, we may receive your name, email address and profile picture. Guest users may use the game without providing these details.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create player profiles, provide login services and support multiplayer gameplay.

Data Storage

Player information and game data are stored using Firebase services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Authentication providers and service providers may process information as necessary to provide the game.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us through the contact method provided in the game.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the PISHPIRIK developer through the app.

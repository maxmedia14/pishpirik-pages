Request Account and Data Deletion

Users of PISHPIRIK can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Please contact the PISHPIRIK developer and include the email address associated with your account or your player ID.

What will be deleted

We will delete the associated player profile and personal information stored by PISHPIRIK, where applicable.

Processing

We will review and process valid deletion requests. Some information may be retained where required by law.
